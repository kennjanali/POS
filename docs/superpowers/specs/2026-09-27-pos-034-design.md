## 1. Goal

Evolve the existing POS@034 build into **POS-034**: a simple, offline-first point of sale with
inventory, orders/sales and printed slips. It is **one general POS** for any small shop — a
sari-sari store, a hardware store, an eatery, a repair shop, a car wash: Inventory holds whatever
the shop sells, goods and services alike, and a handful of switches turn on what a shop needs.
The vendor installs it on an Android tablet.

POS-034 is the first app sold under **kennali.com**, a shop of simple business apps. The platform
behind it (accounts, licensing, backups, updates, vendor dashboard) is shared by future apps.

### Success criteria
- One app, one code base, one license type serves every kind of shop.
- Selling, stock, quotes, reports and daily close all work with no internet.
- Stock can never go below zero, anywhere.
- No closed sale, payment or stock movement is ever lost or half-saved (crash, force-kill, flat battery).
- A replacement tablet is fully restored from the encrypted cloud backup with the license key + recovery code.
- A prospect can see the POS running a shop like theirs in the web demo in under a minute.

### Decisions (fixed)

|---|---|
| 1 | **Evolve this repo.** No rewrite. Keep the offline engine, users/roles, void-never-delete, audit, tax engine, license, backups, printing, daily close |
| 2 | **One general app.** No shop types or presets (Revision 2): every shop starts on the same switches and turns on what it needs |
| 3 | **Pricing: one-time license per tablet + optional yearly maintenance** (updates + support). No sales-percentage fee, no subscription |
| 4 | **Android tablet only** for v1, Bluetooth thermal printer |

| 11 | **Two roles.** The owner runs inventory, prices and discounts; staff take orders, take payment, make quotes and see today's sales |

## 3. Switches (one general POS)

There are no shop types and no presets. Every shop starts on the same default switches, and the
owner changes any of them in Settings → More options. Every behaviour is gated by a switch; nothing
depends on what kind of business it is. Inventory always holds products **and** services.

| Switch | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `openOrders` | off | Sales stay open until paid (tables, jobs, a queue), side by side |
| `serveStep` | off | Lines are served before payment; food order types (dine-in, takeout, delivery) |
| `quotes` | on | Quotations (§6.2) |
| `vehiclePlate` | off | A plate number on open sales and slips |
| `measuredUnits` | on | Decimal quantities for m and kg (2.5 m, 0.75 kg) |

- **Ticket word:** open sales are called "Sale" unless the owner renames them (e.g. "Table",
  "Job", "Queue") under More options.
- **Order types** follow the switches: with a serve step, dine-in / takeout / delivery; otherwise
  walk-in / pickup / delivery.
- The four niche **demo businesses** (§9) show the same POS with different switches turned on.
- **No Senior/PWD discount feature** (removed 2026-09-27). Discounts come from the owner only: promo codes (%) or an owner discount (% / ₱), see §6.2a.
  VAT is shop-level and automatic (§6.2b).

## 3a. Easy by default

Principle: **a new owner is selling within 10 minutes of opening the app, and staff learn it in
one shift.** Anything advanced stays hidden until someone looks for it.

- **Setup asks 3 things** (§6.3). Everything else sits on a **"Finish setting up"** checklist card
  on the home screen (printer, address and contact, VAT, license, add staff), done whenever the
  owner is ready. The card disappears when every item is done or dismissed.
- **VAT defaults to "not VAT-registered".** The slip, screens and reports then show no VAT at all.
  Switching VAT registration on (owner, from the checklist or Settings) makes VAT appear automatically
  on every new sale (§6.2b).
- **Feature switches** live under **Settings → More options**.
- **Selling:** big tiles, one tap to add; tap a cart line to change the quantity or remove it.
  Payment buttons: **Exact cash**, **GCash**, and **Other / split** (all methods, split payments,
  change calculation). One **Discount** button: anyone can enter a **promo code**; only the owner
  sees the manual % / ₱ option.
- **Quick-add to Inventory** (owner only): if an item isn't in the list, the owner adds it to
  Inventory from the sell screen (name, price, kind/unit defaulting to stock/pcs, opening qty)
  without leaving the sale. It is a normal Inventory item from then on. There are no one-off lines.
- **Plain words on every button and label.** "Sale", "Add stock", "Today", "Practice
  mode", "Cancel sale" (not "void"), "Today's summary" (not "daily close"), "Payment" (not
  "tender"). Internal names in this spec stay as they are; only what the user sees changes.
- **Home screen = Today:** sales, money collected by method, low-stock items (owner). Plus one
  **This month** view (owner). The end-of-day summary runs by itself; the owner only ever
  sees "Today's summary".
- **One-line tips:** the first time a screen opens, it shows one short tip (e.g. "Tap a product to
  add it"). It never shows again for that user.
- Unchanged by choice: PIN sign-in always on; English only.

## 4. Data model

All IDs are UUIDv7. All money is integer centavos. Records marked *append-only* are corrected
by voiding, never by editing or deleting.

**Settings** (changed): business profile (name, address, contact number), VAT registered (the
"prices include VAT" option is removed: Inventory prices always include VAT), the feature switches and ticket word (§3), default reorder level, default quote validity
(days, default 7), receipt footer, paired printer, practice-mode flag.

**Product** (changed)
- `kind`: `stock` | `service`
- `sku` (optional, unique when present), `category`
- `unit`: pcs, set, m, kg, box, hour, serving, etc.
- `priceCents`, `costCents`, `vatExempt`, `active`
- `reorderLevel` (stock only; falls back to the shop default)

**Quantities:** up to 3 decimals when `measuredUnits` is on and the unit is measured; otherwise
whole numbers. Stored as integer **thousandths** (2.5 m → 2500) so quantity maths never uses
floating point. Line total = round-half-up(unit price × qty) to the centavo.

**Customer:** no customer table and no customer accounts. Quotes and open tickets carry an
optional free-text `customerName` and `customerPhone`, printed on the quote or slip.

**Sale** (the current Order, changed; append-only once closed)
- Existing fields keep their meaning: number, status (open / closed / voided), lines, tenders,
  discount, frozen totals, frozen line cost, actor fields, void fields.
- New: `customerName`, `customerPhone`, `vehiclePlate` (all optional text), `fromQuoteId` (optional).
- Discount on a sale is one of: none; **promo** (`promoCodeId`, with `promoCode` and `percent`
  copied and frozen); or **owner discount** (% or fixed ₱, with `discountBy` = owner's user ID).
  Never both.
- Every line references an Inventory item (`productId`); its price comes from that item.
- Lines reference stock or service products. Serve state is used only when `serveStep` is on.
- Removed with the Senior/PWD feature: `diners`, `eligibleDiners`, `discountIdNo`,
  `discountIdName`, and the `senior` / `pwd` discount kinds. Discount kinds become `none` | `promo` | `owner`. Old backups that contain senior/PWD sales still load: their frozen totals are
  kept as-is and shown as "discount (legacy)".
- Payment methods: cash, GCash, Maya, card, bank, other. **No "charge to account" method.** A
  sale closes only when payments equal the total (the existing rule).

**Quotation** (new)
- Own gapless number sequence `Q-0001`, separate from sale numbers.
- `customerName` / `customerPhone` (optional), lines, discount (same rules as a sale; a promo
  code must be valid on the day the quote is made), totals, `validUntil`, status: open / converted /
  expired / cancelled, `convertedSaleId`.
- Never affects stock or sales totals.

**Promo code** (new)
- `code`: 3–12 characters, letters and digits only, stored uppercase, unique (e.g. `GRAND10`).
- `percent`: whole number 1–100. `note` (optional, e.g. "Grand opening"), `active`.
- `startsOn` / `endsOn` (optional dates, Asia/Manila business date). Outside the window = not valid.
- `code` and `percent` are **frozen once the code has been used on a closed sale**; to change them the
  owner deactivates it and creates a new one. Before first use it can be edited.
- Never deleted once used (sales point at it); deactivated instead. Create, edit and deactivate are audited.

**Stock move** (unchanged shape): reason ∈ sale, void, restock, spoilage, count, opening; plus
optional `supplier` / `docNo` text on restocks and an optional new unit cost.

**Stock hold** (derived, not stored): Σ qty of stock lines on **open** sales. Available = on hand − held.

**Daily close** (changed, see §7), **User**, **Audit entry**, **Branch** (one per install):
unchanged except as noted.

### Storage
- One database file on the tablet; one table per record type: settings (kv), products, promos,
  sales, quotes, stockMoves, closes, audit. Each row is stored as JSON under its ID.
- **Atomic multi-table writes (new):** closing a sale writes the sale, its stock moves and the
  audit entry in **one transaction**. Voids, stock receipts, counts and quote
  conversions do the same. Today each table is written separately, which lets a crash leave a
  sale without its stock moves.
- Web demo keeps browser storage behind the same interface.
- Autosave on every change, as today. A failed write is always shown in the top bar.

## 5. Stock rules

**Rule: available stock may never go below zero.** Enforced in the business core, not only in the UI.

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Add to cart / open order | Can't exceed available (on hand − held). The tile shows "Only N left"; 0 = greyed "Out of stock" |
| Open sales (tables, jobs, queue) | Stock lines **hold** their quantity while the sale is open. Removing a line or cancelling the sale releases it |
| Close / serve | Rechecked inside the same transaction as the deduction. If short, nothing is written and the screen names the short line |
| Quote → sale | Quantities checked; short lines must be reduced or removed before converting |
| Spoilage / damage | Can't exceed on hand |
| Stock count | Records the counted quantity (≥ 0) and writes the difference as a move |
| Void | Returns stock (only ever increases it) |
| Service items | Never touch stock |

- A shelf/system mismatch is fixed only by a **stock count or a receipt** (owner only), logged with who and why. There is no override at the counter.
- **Migration:** existing negative balances (from practice data) are reset by a logged opening count when the new build first runs. Practice data is otherwise wiped at go-live anyway.

**Low stock**
- Tile badge "low" when available ≤ reorder level (staff see it too, so they know what is running out).
- A notification to the owner when a sale or move takes a product to or below its reorder level.
- A Low stock / Out of stock list on the owner's Today screen and in Inventory, most urgent first, with an Add stock shortcut.

### Inventory screen (owner only)
One place for everything that is sold and what it costs:
- **Products** (stock items): name, SKU, category, unit, price, cost, reorder level, on hand,
  history; Add stock, Stock count, Damage / spoilage.
- **Services** (never stocked): name, category, unit (job, hour, set…), price. Tiers are separate
  items: "Vulcanize – small tire", "Vulcanize – large tire", "Labor – per hour".
- **Promo codes** (§6.2a).
- **Import / export** (CSV, §6.4) and the low-stock list.

Staff never open Inventory; on the sell screen they see stock only as "Only N left" / "Out of stock".

**Add stock** (receive stock, owner only): pick products, quantity each, optional new unit cost, optional supplier and
document number. A new cost applies to **future** sales only; past sales keep their frozen cost.

## 6. Flows

### 6.1 Sell
Product grid (category tabs + search by name/SKU) and a cart. Tap to add; measured units open a
qty pad. Discount: promo code (anyone) or owner discount (owner only), one per sale (§6.2a).
Pay: Exact cash / GCash / Other-split (§3a), always the full amount. Complete = number assigned,
totals frozen, stock deducted, slip printed, all in one transaction.

**Pricing labor and services (decided).** Every price comes from Inventory:
- The owner sets services up in Inventory with fixed prices, split into tiers where the job varies:
  "Vulcanize – tire (small)", "Vulcanize – tire (large)", "Labor – per hour ₱300" sold × 2 for two
  hours, "Alignment ₱600".
- A job that can only be priced after inspection: the owner adds the right service (or tier) to
  Inventory, then it is quoted or sold like any other item.
- Staff never type a price. If a price is wrong, the owner fixes it in Inventory; past sales keep
  the price they closed at.

- **Default (`openOrders` off):** quick sale: cart → pay → next. No open sales.
- **`openOrders` on:** open a sale (a table, job or queue ticket; customer and plate optional).
  Several open side by side; stock is held while open; deducted at pay, or at serve when
  `serveStep` is on.
- **Cancel sale (void):** owner only, reason required. Stock returned.

### 6.2 Quotations
Save the cart as a quote → `Q-` number, valid for the default number of days. Print it on the
receipt printer marked **"QUOTATION — not a receipt"**, or share it as an image. Convert → goes to
payment, or becomes an open sale when `openOrders` is on. Quoted prices are honoured.
Converting an expired quote warns first. "Re-quote" copies it at current prices.

### 6.2a Promo codes
- **Owner creates** codes in Inventory → Promo codes: code, %, optional note and dates. The list
  shows each code's status (active / scheduled / expired / off) and how many sales used it.
- **Applying:** at payment, tap Discount → type the code. Checked on the tablet (works offline):
  it must exist, be active, and be inside its dates. A wrong or expired code says so and changes
  nothing.
- **Maths:** percent off the sale's gross, rounded half-up to the centavo, through the existing
  custom-discount path of the tax engine (so VAT is recomputed correctly on VAT-registered shops).
  100% makes the sale ₱0.00; it still closes, is numbered and deducts stock.
- **One discount per sale.** Entering a code replaces an owner discount and vice versa (after a
  confirm). Removing the discount after a payment was entered needs the payment re-entered (the
  existing "payments must equal the total" rule).
- **Frozen:** the sale keeps the code and percent it closed with; deactivating or expiring the code
  later changes nothing on past sales or open quotes.
- **Slip:** "Promo GRAND10 (10%) −₱50.00".
- **Reports (owner):** This month shows each promo code's uses and total discount given.

### 6.2b VAT (shop-level, automatic)
- **One setting, owner only:** VAT-registered yes / no. Default no. Changing it is audited
  (`settings.vat`) and affects only sales closed from then on; past sales keep their frozen VAT.
- **Prices always include VAT.** On a VAT-registered shop, VAT = price after discount ÷ 1.12 × 0.12,
  worked out automatically on every sale. Example: ₱1,120 sale → VAT ₱120.00 included; with a 10%
  promo → ₱1,008 due, VAT ₱108.00 included.
- **No per-sale VAT switch.** Nobody can leave VAT out of a single sale, so the app's totals always
  agree with the shop's BIR invoices.
- **Seen everywhere (VAT-registered shops):** the slip prints "VAT (12%) included ₱120.00"; Sales history
  has a VAT column; Today, This month and Today's summary show VAT collected. Non-VAT shops see none of it.
- The BIR invoice stays the legal document; the slip remains "Not an official receipt".

### 6.3 Setup wizard
Three questions, then the recovery code (Revision 2 — it never asks what kind of shop it is):
1. Shop name
2. Owner name + PIN ×2
3. What to start with: a small sample catalog (everyday goods and a few services) / import CSV /
   start empty

Then the **recovery code** is shown once (printable later from the checklist). The app opens in
**practice mode** on the Today screen, with the "Finish setting up" checklist: pair printer + test
print, address and contact number, VAT, activate license (needed to go live), add staff.

The existing "restore from cloud backup" path stays available on the first screen.

### 6.4 CSV import / export (owner only)
Template columns: `name, sku, category, kind, unit, price, cost, reorder_level, opening_qty`.
A preview comes before any write ("312 rows, 3 errors: row 14 price missing"). Rows with errors
are fixed or skipped. Matching SKU = update, never a duplicate. Opening qty is written as `opening`
moves. Export uses the same format.

## 7. Daily sales and daily close

The Today screen and Today's summary (the daily close) show two blocks:

- **Sales:** count, gross, discounts (promo codes and owner discounts shown separately), net sales, cancelled sales (count + amount).
- **VAT collected** (VAT-registered shops only).
- **Money collected:** by payment method, and the total.
- **Expected cash in drawer** = cash from sales; optional counted cash and the difference.

The daily close (auto at 23:59 or next launch; manual + printable) freezes both blocks. Windows,
the carrying forward of voids, and the hash chain all work as today. The close record gains
`promoDiscountCents`, `ownerDiscountCents`, `vatCents`, `expectedCashCents` and optional `countedCashCents`,
and these are in the hash.
Each close also stores a `format` number, and the hash covers the fields of its own format, so
closes written by the current build still verify after the upgrade.

Today / This month extras (owner): net, best sellers, gross profit (frozen cost), low-stock list.

## 8. Roles

Two roles.

| | Owner (`superadmin`) | Staff (`staff`) |
|---|---|---|
| Take orders: sell, open tickets, add/remove lines before payment | ✓ | ✓ |
| Take payment and print the slip | ✓ | ✓ |
| Make and print quotations; convert a quote to a sale | ✓ | ✓ |
| See Today's sales and money collected | ✓ | ✓ |
| Apply a promo code | ✓ | ✓ |
| Create / edit / deactivate promo codes; manual discount | ✓ | — |
| Cancel a sale or quote | ✓ | — |
| **Inventory:** add stock, count, spoilage/damage, low-stock list | ✓ | — |
| **Prices, products and services** in Inventory: edit, quick-add, CSV import/export | ✓ | — |
| This month, reports, settings, users, switches | ✓ | — |

- Staff see stock only as the "Only N left" / "Out of stock" labels on the sell screen.
- **Migration:** the stored keys `waiter` and `purchaser` both become `staff` when the new build
  first loads; `superadmin` is unchanged. Old backups load the same way.
- All checks go through the existing `can()` matrix.

## 12. Testing and launch gates

**Automated checks** (extend `npm run check`):
- Tax: VAT-registered (prices include VAT) and non-VAT shops, promo % and owner % / fixed ₱ discounts (₱1,120 → VAT ₱120.00; with 10% promo → ₱1,008 due, VAT ₱108.00). The Senior/PWD cases are removed with the feature.
- Decimal quantities: 2.5 m × ₱38.40 = ₱96.00, and rounding at the half-centavo.
- Stock: can't sell beyond available; holds across two open tickets; recheck on close; count/spoilage can't go below 0; a void restores.
- Atomicity: a simulated failure mid-close leaves neither the sale nor its moves.
- Roles: staff are refused manual discounts, cancels, Inventory access, stock changes, price/product/promo edits, reports and settings, in the core and on screen.
- Promo codes: unknown, inactive, not-yet-started and expired codes refused; 10% of ₱500.00 = ₱50.00; rounding at the half-centavo; VAT-registered shop recomputes VAT; 100% closes at ₱0.00; one discount per sale; code/percent frozen after first use; past sales unchanged after deactivation.
- Quotes: numbering gapless and separate; convert honours prices; a short-stock convert is blocked.
- Daily close: sales, discounts (promo vs owner), collected by method, expected cash, chain intact / tamper detected.
- CSV: preview errors, SKU update, no duplicates, opening moves.
- License (with `app`), backups (seal/open, wrong code), the existing auth checks.
- Server: activation, second-tablet refusal, heartbeat without sales data, backup round trip.
- Migration: a current-build backup loads into the new build (products get `kind: stock`; `waiter`/`purchaser` become `staff`).

**On a real tablet + printer** (before any customer):
- Opens in airplane mode.
- Kill the app mid-sale 20× → nothing lost or half-saved.
- Reboot → intact.
- Printer off/on reconnects.
- Slips print correctly at 58 mm and 80 mm.
- Restore onto a factory-reset tablet.
- Each demo business loads and sells.

**Before launch** (non-code):
- Tax practitioner confirms the "not an official receipt" slip wording.
- EULA + privacy notice (Data Privacy Act, RA 10173).
- Vendor's own business registration and receipts for license sales.
