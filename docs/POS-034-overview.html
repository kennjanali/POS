POS-034 — Build Brief

Offline POS for small shops, by kennali.com. One tablet = one shop. Status: designed, not built. Written 2026-09-27. Full detail: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-pos-034-design.md (spec) and docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-27-pos-034-app.md (18 tasks).

1. What it is

Sell
Inventory → orders → payment → printed slip. Works with no internet.
Any small shop
Restaurant, hardware, auto parts & service, car wash, general — via presets.
Owner in control
Only the owner sets stock, prices, discounts, promo codes and VAT.
Pay once
One-time license per tablet + optional yearly updates. No sales fee.

2. Fixed rules

  • Evolve the existing repo — no rewrite.
  • Stock never goes below zero. Open orders hold stock; low-stock alerts to the owner.
  • No utang / credit. Every sale is paid in full.
  • No Senior/PWD discount. Discounts = owner promo codes (%) or an owner discount (% / ₱). One per sale.
  • Every item sold exists in Inventory at the owner's price. Nothing typed at the counter.
  • VAT is shop-level and automatic: VAT-registered yes/no. Prices include VAT. No per-sale switch.
  • Money in whole centavos; quantities in thousandths (2.5 m = 2500).
  • Void-never-delete, gapless sale numbers (given at payment), totals frozen at payment, audit log.
  • Every change saved immediately, as one all-or-nothing batch.
  • Slip says “Not an official receipt”. Not a BIR machine. Android tablet only.

9. Done means

  • npm run check passes (typecheck, lint, tests).
  • On a tablet: opens in airplane mode; kill the app mid-sale → nothing half-saved; reboot → data intact; slips print at 58 and 80 mm; each demo business loads and sells.
  • Before launch: tax practitioner checks slip wording; EULA + privacy notice; your own business registration.
