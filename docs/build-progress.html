What the redesign changed

The 2026-09-27 spec supersedes the product direction in REWORK-PLAN.md (restaurant-only, 2% fee) and PROJECT-BRIEF.md. Those files stay as history.

  • Dropped: the 2% technology fee and all server-side billing; the Senior/PWD statutory discount; multi-branch.
  • Added: shop-type presets (restaurant, hardware, auto, car wash, general) as feature switches; promo codes; quotations; product kind / SKU / category; decimal quantities.
  • Kept: the offline engine, void-never-delete, the audit trail, PINs and the recovery code, the permission matrix, licensing, backups, printing, the daily close.
  • New rules: stock can never go below zero; one discount per sale and it comes from the owner; no price is ever typed at the counter; every sale is paid in full — no credit.
